Solarpunk Valley

One settlement against five computer players over 12 seasons, about 6 minutes of play.

  • Planning phase each season: vote, research, build
  • Settlers, needs and the Doughnut shared goal
  • Crises, mutual aid and the Skysail
  • Rebalanced from about 1,700 simulated sessions
