Untitled multiplayer solarpunk game · isometric aesthetic study

A painted solarpunk valley

A small isometric mock-up aiming at the hand-painted, Ghibli-like feel of Dear Alice and the Cole Hastings solarpunk art: warm light, big skies, lush greenery and gentle tech. It is painted procedurally in code, so treat it as a direction for mood, light and composition, not final art.

Earlier direction sketches

The four rough directions from before. The plan is to mix and match them per setting, e.g. Earth & Pattern for a dry map and Clean Bright Utopia for another.

Mixes worth considering

  • Retrofit Rebirth that grows into Art Nouveau Green. The map starts as reclaimed concrete and patched panels. As players restore it, the architecture gets more organic and ornate, so progress shows in how the world looks.
  • Earth & Pattern with Clean Bright Utopia tech. Rammed earth and woven textiles, with sleek solar glass and wind turbines added on top. Warm and grounded, and it still reads as the future.
  • One look per region. Each player community or biome develops its own style, which gives multiplayer groups a visual identity on the shared map.
